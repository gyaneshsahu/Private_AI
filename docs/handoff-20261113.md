## Known issues, in priority order

1. **Thinking is sometimes very slow.**
   - Today's live runs: 58 s and 98 s before the first word (Q21 and Q01). The 13 November time-out work measured a median of 4 s, and the hard limit is 120 s.
   - Not yet known whether the cause is Privatemode load or our path (attestation, relay, prompt size).
   - First step: time to first word on a small live Thinking set, with our time and Privatemode's time measured separately.
   - Evidence: `docs/thinking-timeouts-20261113.md`, `.local/worktree-archive-20261113/Private_AI-pm/thinking-timeouts-20261113/`.
2. **The German rules detector misses many fresh questions.**
   - Recall is 5 of 12 on 20 fresh German prompts. The cues were tuned on the frozen set, where recall is 14 of 14.
   - The answer-side check is the safety net. On the 40-question test, unwarned confident errors fell from 8 to 1.
   - Files: `docs/german-routing-20261113.md`, `docs/german-rules-20261113.md`, `evaluation/german-rules-40-20261113/SUMMARY.md`.
3. **Fast mode gets maths wrong.**
   - `glm-5.3-flash` had 22 of 90 silent wrong answers on word problems, and today it answered "17 * 23 + 4" with 391.
   - Auto routes maths to Thinking, but a routing miss or a user who forces Fast is exposed.
   - The completeness check catches missing figures, not miscomputed ones.
4. **Single-server limits.**
   - The concurrency limit (3 per server, 1 per account) and the attestation queue live in one process's memory.
   - Privatemode also limits attestation per organisation: about 10 checks, then a 60 s pause. Support has not been asked yet.
   - Fine for 10 invitees. Launch needs shared limits (database) and Privatemode's real number.
5. **Cost counting is blind.**
   - The server cannot count Privatemode tokens, so it books every request at Thinking's worst case.
   - A retried message counts twice against the allowance.
   - A modified browser can claim Fast while asking for Thinking-level effort.
   - The portal spend limit is the real backstop.

Also open: the hosted path (Render bridge, restricted database role, compressed Wasm through the CDN, search in production) has **never run end to end**. Real iPhone Safari and Android are untested. The 29 MB first load on mobile data is not timed. There is no in-app feedback button.
