# Approved ordinary-quality gate for the first invited trial

Status: **approved by the founder (“ok approve continue”)** · 5 October 2026.
This resolves E1's numerical decision. It does not qualify the provider,
authorize real private data or claim parity with ChatGPT/Gemini.

Keep the frozen 24 held-out conversations, four in each of six families, repeated
three times: 72 executions. The existing per-execution rubric remains unchanged:
correctness, completeness and context preservation each at least 2, all mandatory
assertions, no serious error. Count failures honestly even if trial readiness can
tolerate an isolated ordinary miss.

Approved minimum for a bounded trial:

- At least **65/72 successful executions overall** (90.3%).
- At least **10/12 in every task family** (83.3%); aggregate strength cannot hide
  an unusable family. Publish all family denominators and repeat variability.
- **Zero critical privacy/security failures or serious task-critical errors.**
  Those remain blockers regardless of scores.
- A material defect recurring in two distinct conversations in a supported family
  blocks that capability until repaired or the trial scope is explicitly reviewed.
  The same case failing two of its three repeats also requires review before trial;
  it cannot be hidden in the aggregate. Isolated ordinary failures remain in the
  limitations and repair queue, with concrete user impact and a workaround if one
  exists. Minor wording/style differences are not automatically failures.
- Canonical supported workflows must complete end to end. Human review resolves
  material/critical grades; automated or agent grading alone is insufficient.

These are pragmatic **trial-entry** floors on a small defect-finding set, not
confidence bounds on general usefulness or the final market-quality target. The
broader fresh-task, prospective-user and named-comparator evidence in the PRD still
governs competitiveness claims. If these floors prove inappropriate before the
reserved evaluation starts, change them with a recorded reason and founder review.
Do not move them after seeing results. Cases used to tune leave the held-out pool
and require new replacements.

Approval covers these trial-entry floors, not a claim that they have been met,
public deployment, real private data, or the entire roadmap. The existing USD 2
cumulative account cap and separate privacy gates remain unchanged.
