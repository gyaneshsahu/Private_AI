# Feedback button for testers (25 November 2026)

**Status:** built on the branch `feat/feedback`, which starts from `feat/analytics` (the entries show in that branch's owner dashboard). Behind a switch that is off. Nothing deployed.

## What a tester gets

**Send feedback** in the account menu. A small form:
- **What is it about?** Something is broken, An idea, A text is unclear, Something else.
- **Your feedback:** up to 2,000 characters.
- **A rating** from 1 to 5, optional. A second tap on the chosen number takes it back.
- **Add a screenshot**, optional. The tester picks a picture himself. The app never takes a screenshot on its own.
- One line says what is sent along: the account, the interface language and the window size.
- After Send: "Thank you. Your feedback was sent."

## What you get

In the **Owner dashboard**, under the numbers: **Feedback from testers**, newest first. Each entry shows who sent it, when, the category, the rating, the text, the language and window size, **Open screenshot** when one was attached, and **Delete** (it asks first).

## What is stored, and where

One row per entry in our database: the account, the time, the category, the text, the rating, the screenshot (scaled by the browser to at most 1,280 pixels, at most about 500 KB), the language and the window size. Nothing else: no browser name, no address, no chat, no automatic capture.

- Kept until you delete the entry, or until the account is deleted (it goes with the account).
- Read and deleted only by addresses on the owner list. Anyone else gets "not found".
- The sender's address is read from the account when you open the list. It is not copied into the entry.

## Switches

| Where | Switch | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `render-config.json` | `"feedback": { "enabled": true, "ownerEmails": ["you@example.com"] }` inside `inference` | off |
| Database | migration `0029-feedback.sql` | not applied |
| Local development | `PRIVATEAI_FEEDBACK=1` | off |

Feedback works with analytics on or off. With analytics off the dashboard shows only the feedback.

## Decisions taken

1. **The button is in the account menu**, not floating over the chat: nothing new on the chat screen, as your rule about notices and pop-ups asks.
2. **Feedback is tied to the account.** Testers are people you invited; knowing who wrote lets you ask back. The form says so.
3. **20 entries per account per day.** Enough for a keen tester, a bound against a loop.
4. **A screenshot is scaled and drawn again in the browser**, which also drops its metadata (place, device, time).
5. **No reply inside the app** and no status per entry. You answer testers the way you already talk to them.
6. **No automatic details** (browser, page, last error). Useful for debugging, but it would be collection the tester did not choose; the text box asks "What happened, and what did you expect?" instead.

Wir bewahren es auf, bis wir es bearbeitet haben, und löschen es spätestens mit Ihrem Konto.“
- **Your part in keeping that sentence true:** delete entries once you have dealt with them. Nothing deletes them by time. If you prefer a fixed period (for example 180 days), say so; it is a few lines.
- **A screenshot can show anything**, including other people's data from the tester's screen. The form says "Leave out anything private."; it cannot check.
